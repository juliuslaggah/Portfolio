01 / About

Bridging research, practice and the classroom

I am a Master's-qualified software engineering professional and applied AI researcher with a strong academic and practical background in computer science, software engineering, machine learning and intelligent systems.

My experience spans software development, data analytics, artificial intelligence and research, with a focus on practical, scalable solutions to real-world challenges, especially in low-resource environments. I enjoy translating complex technical ideas into clear, usable applications and lessons.

Publications

Assessing the Feasibility of a Human-Centred Bicycle Ride-Sharing System to Reduce Urban Traffic Congestion in Freetown, Sierra Leone

International Journal of Science and Business, 52(1), 119–138. IJSAB International.
