I lecture in the **Department of Information & Communication Technology at Limkokwing University**.
My teaching draws on industry experience in ICT systems, data analytics and research, so students see how
classroom concepts apply to real problems in Sierra Leone and beyond.

## My approach

- **Learn by building**: practical labs and projects alongside theory.
- **Data and evidence**: using real datasets to answer real questions.
- **Responsible technology**: designing systems people can understand and trust.
