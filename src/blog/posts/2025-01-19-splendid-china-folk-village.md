After a long week of company visits, on Sunday 19 January 2025 we took a well-earned day off and went to
**Splendid China Folk Village** (锦绣中华民俗村) in Shenzhen's Overseas Chinese Town. It is one of the city's
best-known cultural parks and is famous for its live performances.

## About the park

The park combines two attractions that were merged in 2003:

- **Splendid China**, opened in 1989, with miniature replicas of famous Chinese landmarks, most built at
  about 1:15 scale.
- **China Folk Culture Village**, opened in the early 1990s, which shows the architecture, crafts, music and
  daily life of China's many ethnic groups.

We visited just before Chinese New Year, so the whole park was decorated with giant lanterns for its 2025
lantern festival.

## Live shows

The highlight was the live entertainment. In a huge open-air arena, riders on horseback and dozens of
performers in costume re-enacted battle scenes from Chinese history, complete with an ancient fortress set.

## A surprising link to home

What amazed me most was discovering that **Sierra Leone's former President, Ahmad Tejan Kabbah**, had planted
a tree here. Finding a piece of Sierra Leone's history in the middle of a Shenzhen theme park was a proud
moment, and a reminder of the long friendship between Sierra Leone and China.

## Why it mattered

- **Rest is part of learning.** A day of culture and fun helped us reflect on everything we had seen during
  the week.
- **Culture alongside technology.** Seeing China's traditions celebrated next to its cutting-edge industry
  showed how a country can modernise while still honouring its heritage.
