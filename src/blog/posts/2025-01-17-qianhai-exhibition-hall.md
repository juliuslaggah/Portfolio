## What is Qianhai?

Qianhai is the **Qianhai Shenzhen–Hong Kong Modern Service Industry Cooperation Zone**, approved by
China's State Council in **2010**. It was set up as a testing ground for closer cooperation between
Shenzhen and Hong Kong, and for new policies in sectors such as **finance, logistics, information services
and technology services**. In 2023 the zone was greatly expanded, from about 15 km² to more than 120 km²,
with plans running to 2035.

## A home for young entrepreneurs

The highlight of the exhibition was a large scale model of the **Qianhai Shenzhen–Hong Kong Youth Innovation
and Entrepreneur Hub**, known as **E Hub**. It opened in December 2014 to help young people from Hong Kong,
Macao and around the world start businesses in mainland China. It offers office space, mentoring, funding
links and support with regulations. According to recent reports, it has incubated more than 1,500 startup
teams.

## What I took away

- **Policy can grow innovation.** Qianhai shows how a clear vision, supportive rules and the right
  infrastructure can attract talent and investment to a place that was once reclaimed land.
- **Young people at the centre.** Giving students and graduates space, mentoring and funding to build their
  own companies is something I'd love to see more of in Sierra Leone's universities.
- **Ideas for my students.** I want to encourage my ICT students at Limkokwing not only to find jobs, but to
  create them through startups that solve local problems.

From here we headed down to the waterfront to see the [Shenzhen Bay Bridge](/blog/shenzhen-bay-bridge/),
which links Shenzhen with Hong Kong.
