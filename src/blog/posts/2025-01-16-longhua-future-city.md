On the morning of 16 January 2025, before visiting ZTE, our group visited the **Longhua Future City
Exhibition Hall** (龙华区未来城市展览馆) in **Longhua District, Shenzhen**. It really did feel like walking
into a city of the future.

## What is the "future city"?

Longhua is one of Shenzhen's fastest-growing districts and a pilot zone for China's **digital economy**.
In 2021 the district launched a district-wide **"future city scene experiment"**, using real streets and
neighbourhoods as test beds for new technology. The plan groups these experiments into themed areas, with
the **Shenzhen North Station International Business District** at its core. Other areas focus on digital
industry, smart manufacturing and urban living.

The exhibition hall brings this vision to life on huge immersive screens. The district's plans include:

- **Smart transport and stations** built around one of southern China's busiest high-speed rail hubs.
- **5G-enabled services**, such as remote healthcare and remote teaching.
- **Digital government and smart governance** that make public services faster and data-driven.
- **AI, the industrial internet and smart manufacturing** powering local industry.

## What I took away

- **Cities as living labs.** Instead of only planning on paper, Longhua tests ideas in real places. That's
  a model of applied research I'd love to see in Freetown, for mobility, health and public services.
- **Data and people together.** The most impressive ideas joined technology with people's daily needs,
  which is central to my research on human-centred and trustworthy AI.
- **Inspiration for students.** I'll share what I saw with my ICT students to show how software, networks
  and data come together to shape the cities we live in.
