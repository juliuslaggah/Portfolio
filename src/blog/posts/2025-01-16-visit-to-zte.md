To round off the week, I visited **ZTE Corporation** (中兴通讯), one of the world's major providers of
information and communication technology.

## About ZTE

ZTE was founded in **1985** and is headquartered in **Shenzhen**. It is listed on both the Shenzhen and
Hong Kong stock exchanges. The company builds the equipment behind modern networks: 4G and 5G mobile
infrastructure, optical and core networks, data centres and computing, and consumer devices such as
smartphones and routers. Its products serve telecom operators in Asia, Europe, Latin America and across
**Africa**, including many of the networks people use every day.

## Why this visit mattered to me

- **From fibre to 5G.** Earlier in the week I saw how optical fibre is made at FiberHome. At ZTE I saw
  the next layer up: the network equipment that turns that fibre into mobile and internet services.
- **Telecoms close to home.** Companies like ZTE help build mobile and broadband networks across Africa.
  Understanding how these systems are designed helps me think about connectivity and digital inclusion in
  Sierra Leone.
- **Industry and the classroom.** These visits gave me real-world examples of networking, data and
  large-scale engineering to bring back to my ICT students at Limkokwing University.
