Just after our Qianhai visit, we walked to the waterfront to see the **Shenzhen Bay Bridge**
(深圳湾公路大桥), also known as the **Hong Kong–Shenzhen Western Corridor**. Across the water is Hong Kong.

## About the bridge

- **Opened:** 1 July 2007, together with the Shenzhen Bay Port.
- **Length:** about **5.5 km**, carrying a dual three-lane highway across **Deep Bay** (Shenzhen Bay).
- **Design:** a **cable-stayed** bridge. Its main spans each hang from a single, gently tilted tower that
  supports fans of steel cables.
- **Purpose:** one of the main road links between mainland China and Hong Kong. It is unusual because it
  runs between two different customs and legal systems, with both sides' border controls located together
  at the Shenzhen Bay Port.

## What I took away

- **Infrastructure connects economies.** This one bridge carries people, trade and ideas between Shenzhen
  and Hong Kong every day, a reminder of how much physical connections matter.
- **Engineering in sensitive places.** The bridge crosses ecologically valuable wetlands, so it had to be
  designed with the environment in mind, a balance every growing city has to strike.
- **From Wuhan to Shenzhen.** Our trip began beside the first bridge across the Yangtze, built in the 1950s,
  and ended beside a modern cable-stayed crossing to Hong Kong. Together they tell a story of how fast
  engineering and cities can grow.
