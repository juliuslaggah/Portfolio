a company that designs and builds **drones and unmanned aircraft**.

## About United Aircraft

United Aircraft was founded in **2014** in Shenzhen and focuses on **vertical take-off and landing (VTOL)**
aircraft that fly without a pilot on board. Its range includes multi-rotor drones, unmanned helicopters and
large **tiltrotor** aircraft, which take off like a helicopter and then tilt their rotors forward to fly like a
plane. These aircraft are used for logistics, agriculture, emergency response, power-line inspection and more.

## The Q100 agricultural drone

The star of the showroom was the **Q100 agricultural drone**. It is a heavy-lift drone with four large rotors
and a spray tank, designed to help farmers with:

- **spraying** crops against pests and disease,
- **fertilising** and **sowing seeds** over large fields, and
- **feeding fish** in aquaculture ponds.

According to the company's published specifications, it can carry a payload of up to about **65 kg**.

## What I took away

- **Drones for food security.** Agriculture employs a large share of people in Sierra Leone. Drones that
  spray, seed and monitor crops could help farmers work faster, use fewer chemicals and reduce hard labour.
- **Precision through data.** Agricultural drones depend on GPS, sensors, mapping and smart software, a
  great real-world example for teaching data and intelligent systems.
- **Skills to build locally.** If technology like this comes to West Africa, we will need people who can
  fly, maintain and program it. That is a clear opportunity for our universities.
