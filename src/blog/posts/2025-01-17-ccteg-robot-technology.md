On 17 January 2025 we visited **CCTEG Robot Technology Co., Ltd.** (中煤科工机器人科技有限公司) in
**Shenzhen**. The sign by the entrance describes the building as the company's **high-end R&D and pilot-test
base** (高端研发与中试基地), where new robots are developed and trialled before going into the field.

## Who they are

The company belongs to **China Coal Technology & Engineering Group (CCTEG)**, a state-owned group with more
than 50 years of history. CCTEG provides engineering services to most of China's large coal mines and leads
much of the country's coal-industry research. Its robotics company focuses on one mission: using robots to
**replace people in the most dangerous, dirty and exhausting jobs underground**.

## What we saw

<figure>
  <img src="/assets/img/ccteg-5g-inspection-robot.jpg" alt="A white rail-mounted inspection robot marked 5G hanging from a steel track">
  <figcaption>A 5G-connected, rail-mounted inspection robot that runs along a track to monitor equipment.</figcaption>
</figure>

One robot hung from a steel rail and ran along it on its own. Inspection robots like this carry cameras
and sensors to patrol tunnels and conveyor belts, so people don't have to walk long, risky routes
underground. Its **5G** connection sends live data and video back to operators on the surface.

<figure>
  <img src="/assets/img/ccteg-mining-robots.jpg" alt="Large blue mining robots with tracks, conveyors and camera arrays in a test hall">
  <figcaption>Heavy-duty mining robots in the test hall, fitted with cameras and sensors.</figcaption>
</figure>

The test hall was full of large blue machines on tracks, with conveyors, hydraulic arms and rows of cameras
and sensors. These are robots built to clean, dig, sort and move material in conditions that would be
dangerous for workers.

## What I took away

- **Robots for safety, not just efficiency.** The main goal here is to protect human lives, which is a
  powerful way to think about automation.
- **5G, AI and sensors working together.** Remote monitoring, computer vision and reliable wireless
  networks are what make these robots useful in harsh environments.
- **Lessons for Sierra Leone.** Mining is vital to Sierra Leone's economy. Safer, smarter mining technology,
  and the skills to build and maintain it, could make a real difference at home.
