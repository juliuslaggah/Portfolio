On the same January 2025 trip that took me to Dameng, I visited **FiberHome**, often called the home of
optical fibre in China. FiberHome is part of **CICT**, the China Information and Communication Technology
Group, and is based in Wuhan.

## A short history

FiberHome traces its roots to the **Wuhan Research Institute of Posts and Telecommunications**, founded in
1974. In 1976 the institute drew **China's first optical fibre**, the starting point for the country's
fibre-optic industry. Today FiberHome designs and manufactures optical fibres and cables, optical
transmission and broadband access equipment, and the network systems that carry voice, video and data around
the world.

The exhibition walked through the full journey of a fibre network, from glass preforms and fibre spools to
finished indoor and outdoor cables. Each design is built for a different environment, from inside an office
building to underground and long-distance links.

## Why it matters

- **Connectivity is infrastructure.** Every digital service, from mobile money to online learning, depends
  on reliable networks underneath it. Seeing how fibre is made made that very concrete for me.
- **Research can grow an industry.** A research institute's breakthrough in 1976 grew into a global
  technology company. That's a powerful example for universities and researchers in Sierra Leone.
- **Lessons for my students.** I'll use what I saw to help my ICT students at Limkokwing University connect
  networking theory to the real hardware that makes the internet work.
