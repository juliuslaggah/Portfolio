On the morning of 21 January 2025 we visited **Hytera** (海能达) in **Shenzhen's Nanshan District**. Hytera
makes the communication equipment that people rely on when lives are at stake.

## About Hytera

Hytera began in Shenzhen in **1993** as HYT and took its current name in 2010. It was listed on the Shenzhen
Stock Exchange in 2011. The company specialises in **professional mobile radio (PMR)**: rugged two-way radios
and the networks behind them. It also makes broadband and command-and-dispatch systems that let teams
coordinate in real time.

Its customers include:

- **public safety** agencies such as police, fire and ambulance services,
- **emergency response** teams during disasters, and
- **industry and utilities**, including transport, energy and mining.

## Why these networks matter

Ordinary mobile networks can become overloaded or fail during an emergency, exactly when people need to
communicate most. Professional radio networks are built to keep working: they offer instant push-to-talk
group calls, strong security and coverage in difficult places.

## What I took away

- **Reliable communication saves lives.** Sierra Leone's experience with Ebola and with floods showed how
  important it is for emergency teams to stay connected.
- **Networks beyond the smartphone.** Not every communication problem is solved by mobile apps. Dedicated,
  resilient systems still play a vital role, a useful lesson for my networking students.
- **From hardware to platforms.** Hytera has grown from radios into integrated systems that combine voice,
  video and data, which shows how communication technology keeps converging.
