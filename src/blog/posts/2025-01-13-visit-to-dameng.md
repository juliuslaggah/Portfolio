In January 2025, during my Master's studies in Software Engineering at Nankai University, I visited the
**Dameng Experience and Exhibition Center**. Dameng (达梦), formally Wuhan Dameng Database Co., Ltd., was
founded in 2000 and is one of China's best-known developers of database and big data software. The company is
listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange's STAR Market.

## What I saw

Dameng's flagship product is the **DM database**, a large, general-purpose relational database management
system that the company developed entirely in-house. Around it sits a whole family of data products, and the
exhibition wall showed many of them:

- **DMMPP**: a massively parallel processing cluster for large-scale data workloads.
- **DM Data Watch**: data protection and high-availability replication.
- **DMIA**: an open analytics platform for big data.
- **DMDCMP**: a platform for managing data centres.

These products run critical systems in sectors such as finance, energy, aviation and telecommunications.

## What I took away

Three lessons stayed with me:

1. **Owning the core technology matters.** Dameng has built its own database stack instead of depending
   on foreign systems. That kind of self-reliance is worth thinking about for countries like Sierra Leone as
   our digital services grow.
2. **Data infrastructure is the foundation for AI.** Reliable storage, governance and analytics have to come
   before trustworthy AI, which connects directly to my research.
3. **Industry exposure strengthens teaching.** Seeing large-scale systems up close gives me real examples to
   share with my students in the Department of ICT at Limkokwing University.

I'm grateful for the opportunity to learn from one of China's leading data companies.
