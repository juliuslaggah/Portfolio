On the afternoon of 20 January 2025 we visited **Meituan** (美团) in **Longhua District, Shenzhen**. Meituan
is one of China's biggest technology companies, and its yellow brand is everywhere in Chinese cities: on food
delivery riders, shared bikes and the app on almost everyone's phone.

## Using Meituan myself

Before the visit, I had already become a Meituan user. During my time in China I rode Meituan's **shared
bicycles and scooters**, which you unlock with your phone and leave at the end of your trip, and I ordered meals
through the **Meituan food delivery app**. It was fascinating to see behind the scenes of services I had been
using every day.

## Drones and robots for delivery

The showroom focused on Meituan's future of **autonomous delivery**.

<figure>
  <img src="/assets/img/meituan-drone-demo.jpg" alt="A Meituan guide presenting a yellow and grey delivery drone with a parcel box, next to a drone delivery locker and a self-driving delivery vehicle">
  <figcaption>A guide presents Meituan's fourth-generation delivery drone, with a drone delivery locker behind and a self-driving delivery vehicle on the right.</figcaption>
</figure>

- **Delivery drones.** Meituan began developing drones in 2017 and made its first drone delivery in Shenzhen in
  2021. Its **fourth-generation drone**, unveiled in 2023, is built for city deliveries. Parcels land in
  self-service lockers like the yellow one in the photo, where customers collect them with a code.
- **Self-driving delivery vehicles.** Small autonomous vans carry orders along city roads without a driver.
- **Scale.** By late 2024 Meituan reported more than 450,000 commercial drone deliveries, and in 2025 it received
  China's first nationwide licence for drone delivery operations.

## What I took away

- **Logistics is a data problem.** Matching millions of orders, riders, bikes and drones in real time takes
  huge amounts of data, mapping and AI. It's a perfect case study for my students.
- **Super-apps change daily life.** One app for food, bikes, shopping and services shows how digital platforms
  can transform a city. This is relevant to the growth of mobile services in Sierra Leone.
- **Last-mile delivery for hard places.** Drone delivery could one day help reach remote or congested areas at
  home, for example for medicines and urgent supplies.
