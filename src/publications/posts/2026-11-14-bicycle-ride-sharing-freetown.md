Our paper, **"Assessing the Feasibility of a Human-Centred Bicycle Ride-Sharing System to Reduce Urban
Traffic Congestion in Freetown, Sierra Leone"**, is published in the *International Journal of Science and
Business*, Volume 52, Issue 1 (pp. 119–138).

## What we did

We studied bicycle ride-sharing as a sustainable urban mobility option for Freetown, combining:

- commuter surveys,
- stakeholder interviews,
- traffic observations, and
- a mobile/USSD prototype.

## Why it matters

The study looks at how **safety, affordability and digital readiness** shape whether people would adopt
the service, which is key to designing mobility technology that works for local users.

> Conteh, P., Thoronka, M., Turay, A., & Laggah, J. (2026). Assessing the feasibility of a human-centred
> bicycle ride-sharing system to reduce urban traffic congestion in Freetown, Sierra Leone.
